Jenna Jang is a Ph.D. student in the Joint Department of Biomedical Engineering at Georgia Tech and Emory University. She received her B.S. in Neuroscience from Vanderbilt University, and was awarded the GEM Fellowship to support her graduate studies. She is interested in investigating the neural circuit mechanisms of vision and experience-dependent plasticity, with a focus on how learning shapes visual processing and adaptive behavior.
